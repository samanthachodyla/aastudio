# Dorsey Brennan — Website Copy Deck

Every word for every page, ready to paste into Squarespace. Written from your own
language in the Canva brand file — the short declarative lines, the line breaks that
read like a letter, "boldly understated," "the art of appreciation."

**Anything in `[BRACKETS]` needs a fact only you have.** Everything else is final.

**House style rules** (keep these and the whole site sounds like one person):
- First person, always. "I source," never "we source."
- Short lines. Break where you'd take a breath, not where the margin runs out.
- Periods on section titles: `Welcome.` `The Philosophy.` `Projects.`
- Place names spelled the old way: **Scranton, Penna.** Never "PA."
- No exclamation points anywhere in site copy. The restraint *is* the brand.
- Never "luxury interiors." You earned "the distinct impression of an intentional life."
