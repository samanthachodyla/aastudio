Welcome.

This started as an antique shop.

Three years in, I felt in my gut that it was time to put my name to this thing, and to cultivate seeds planted at the very beginning.

What you’re seeing now is what my life’s work has all built up to: well-considered, one-of-one, boldly understated interiors that place genuine craftsmanship and creative freedom at the center.

Now offering custom residential projects and hand-chosen vintage.
For those who live by the art of appreciation.

The Philosophy.

This began as an antique shop. Never afraid to be authentic or boldly understated, I’m now going to embrace new materials, too, carefully chosen to support human craft and make tradition approachable for a whole new generation.

It’s intuitive for me that interiors speak volumes in subtlety. You can make things loud, cheap, and chaotic, but I don’t think you should.

Your home should move freely, hold its own, but never cut corners.
