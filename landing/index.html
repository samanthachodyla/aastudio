When does Allegory Studio launch? +
We launch publicly on August 1, 2026. We're in beta now — start your free trial and you'll be set up and ready the moment we go live.
Is there a free trial? +
Yes — every plan includes a 7-day free trial with no credit card required. Explore the whole studio before you decide.
What's the difference between Starter and Pro? +
Starter gives you the core studio: inventory, sales & finance, deadlines, contacts, and the daily dashboard. Pro adds the full Profile Vault, Communications Hub, Marketing Assistant, smart pricing and opportunity discovery — plus direct access to a working arts administrator.
Who is Allegory Studio for? +
Working and emerging artists who are managing the business of their practice — inventory, sales, consignments, exhibitions, and outreach — and want one calm place to do it.
Can I cancel anytime? +
Absolutely. Plans are month-to-month (or annual if you prefer the savings). Cancel anytime, no questions asked.
Is my data private and secure? +
Your studio data is yours. It's stored securely and never sold. You can export or delete it whenever you like.
