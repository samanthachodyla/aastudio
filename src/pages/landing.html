FAQ

When does Allegory Art Studio launch? +
We launch publicly on August 1, 2026. Get first access now and we'll let you know the moment we go live.
What's the difference between Starter and Pro? +
Starter gives you the core studio: inventory, sales & finance, deadlines, contacts, and the daily dashboard. Pro adds the full Profile Vault, Communications Hub, Marketing Assistant, smart pricing and opportunity discovery, plus direct access to a working arts administrator.
Who is Allegory Art Studio for? +
Working and emerging artists who are managing the business of their practice, inventory, sales, consignments, exhibitions, and outreach, and want one calm place to do it. If you're thinking about hiring a studio manager but don't have the budget for one, start with Allegory Art Studio to help offload the admin burden.
Can I cancel anytime? +
Absolutely. Plans are month-to-month (or annual if you prefer the savings). Cancel anytime, no questions asked.
Is my data private and secure? +
Your studio data is yours. It's stored securely and never sold. You can export or delete it whenever you like.
How much does Allegory Art Studio cost? +
Studio Starter is $25/month or $240/year. Studio Pro is $55/month or $528/year. Plans are month-to-month or annual, and you can cancel anytime.
Is there a free trial? +
There's no free trial — Allegory Art Studio is pay-to-start, so you pick a plan when you sign up. Plans are month-to-month or annual, cancel anytime, and discount codes are accepted at checkout.
